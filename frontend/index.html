1. The Challenge We Solve

Context

In space and defense applications, electronics undergo rigorous environmental stress screening at elevated temperatures up to 125°C across hundreds of operational hours.

⚠️ The Hidden Defect Dilemma:
Traditional testing relies solely on static pass or fail thresholds. When subtle internal degradation drifts quietly below standard limits, flawed semiconductors slip through to flight payloads, risking catastrophic failure in orbit.
Real-World Screening Scenario:
• Typical Batch Current: 10 μA
• Anomalous Part Current: 45 μA
• Fixed Datasheet Ceiling: 50 μA
Passes traditional static limits, yet represents an extreme latent flight risk.

2. Our Engineering Solution

Pipeline
🔍
Population Outlier Detection

Instead of looking only at hard ceilings, our system continuously compares each component's drift curve against the entire batch population, flagging parts that behave abnormally compared to healthy peers.

📈
Time-Series Drift Forecast

A gradient boosted regression engine that analyzes initial burn-in telemetry to predict long-term performance at 168 hours. If the predicted degradation curve shows runaway behavior, the part is caught early.

3. Reliability & Explainability

Standards
🛡️ Anomaly Detection Precision
In aerospace missions, missing a defective part is unacceptable. Our screening model heavily penalizes defect escapes to ensure zero faulty components reach flight assembly.
📊 Drift Forecast Accuracy
Trained to minimize prediction error between early burn-in measurements and actual extended lifecycle performance.
🧠 Physics-Grounded Explainability
Powered by Groq Llama 3.3, the system translates mathematical curve shifts into clear semiconductor failure explanations, covering avalanche breakdown, carrier trap recombination, and threshold voltage drift for quality assurance teams.
